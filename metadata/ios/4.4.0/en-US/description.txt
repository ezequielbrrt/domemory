DoMemory is the classic memory card game, played with the emojis you use every day. Flip two cards, find the match, clear the board, and train your memory one game at a time.

ENDLESS LEVELS
Clear a level to unlock the next. Boards grow bigger and the clock gets tighter, and there's always one more level waiting.
• Earn up to 3 stars a level by finishing fast and making few mistakes
• Spend stars on power-ups like Peek, Freeze, Reveal and extra time
• Use stars for an extra life, or to skip a level you're stuck on
• Your lives refill every day

LEVEL SEASONS
Limited-time seasons bring a set of themed levels with their own emojis and artwork. Play through each season before it ends, collect your stars, and look out for the next one.

REAL-TIME MULTIPLAYER
Challenge a friend live. Create a room and share its code or QR code, take turns flipping cards, and see who wins. Then ask for a rematch.

DAILY CHALLENGE
A new board every day. Play it daily to build your streak and keep it going.

AND MORE
• Classic boards in four difficulties: Easy, Medium, Hard and Very hard
• Create your own memoramas with any emojis you like
• Favorite the boards you play most
• Achievements and stats that track your progress
• Made for iPhone and iPad
