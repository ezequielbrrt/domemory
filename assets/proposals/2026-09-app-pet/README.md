# DoMemory app pet proposals

Eight original, full-body transparent sprites explore four different companions. Each has an idle state for recurring UI and a celebration state for wins or streak milestones. `comparison.png` places the idle sprites at the same 340 px canvas scale over the same crop of the real gameplay screenshot. All sprites are 1024 × 1024 RGBA with a true transparent background and at least roughly 10% transparent padding after final framing. The sprite files contain no text or baked effects.

## pet-a — Miso, the axolotl

Miso is a curious coral axolotl who treats every hidden pair as a tiny mystery. The lavender gills give Miso a distinctive silhouette even at small sizes, while the indigo card-shaped belly marking links the character to the app icon. Miso quietly watches the board in the idle state, then raises both arms for a win; that gentle-to-joyful range suits a companion who responds to `totalWon`, `longestStreak`, and the WinModal without pulling attention away from play.

## References and production

- `assets/my-video/public/assets/domemory-app-icon.png`: palette, rounded card motif, shading, and outline treatment.
- `assets/screenshots/6.9/en-US/01_hero_gameplay.jpg`: the real UI backdrop and color harmony test in `comparison.png`.
- `ios/DoMemory/DoMemory/Modules/Settings/SettingsView/AchievementsView.swift`: existing wins, perfect games, streak, and multiplayer progress hooks.
- `ios/DoMemory/DoMemory/Modules/Memorize/MemorizeView/Modals/WinModal/`: natural home for the celebration state.
- Each celebration used its generated idle sprite as the identity reference. Art was generated with the built-in image generation tool. Final transparent sprites were alpha-cropped and fitted into a centered 1024 × 1024 canvas with Pillow; the comparison sheet was composed with Pillow from the gameplay screenshot and idle sprites.

## Recommendation

**Miso (pet-a)** is the strongest starting point. The coral body and indigo card marking connect immediately to the app icon; lavender gills add a recognizable, compact silhouette against the indigo game UI. The curious personality makes discovering pairs feel like shared progress, and the idle/celebrate poses could recur beside streaks and on the WinModal without needing props or text.
