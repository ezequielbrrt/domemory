# DoMemory app pet proposals — round 2

This set treats emoji tiles and matching as the mascots' physical identity. Each concept has an idle sprite for recurring UI and a celebration sprite for wins or streak milestones. All eight sprites are centered, 1024 × 1024 RGBA with real transparent alpha; `comparison.png` places the idle poses at the same canvas scale over the same crop of the actual gameplay screenshot. The art uses DoMemory's coral, indigo, lavender, cream, and amber palette with rounded sticker shapes and no baked text or confetti.

## pet2-a — Flippo, the living reveal card

Flippo is a curious memory card that cannot resist showing what is underneath. The coral question-mark body and indigo backing recall DoMemory's app icon, while a small peeled corner already exposes an apple emoji in the idle pose; the celebration opens the corner wider after a match. A player sees the complete flip-and-reveal loop in the character itself, so Flippo belongs to this emoji card game rather than to an unrelated cute-animal brand. Its single compact silhouette should fit a WinModal or streak header.

Patch is a friendly little figure whose head, torso, and side arm are actual connected emoji tiles: dog, apple, and rocket. The three familiar icons are the same kind of content players flip in DoMemory, and the pastel tile colors echo the live grid. Patch reads as an emoji-game mascot before any animation or copy explains it. Patch could gain new tile motifs as wins accumulate, though the three-icon construction is denser than the other proposals at tiny UI sizes.

## pet2-c — Morphi, the emoji mimic

Morphi is a little lavender chameleon that copies the emoji on the tile nearest it. In idle, half its body becomes the apple emoji shown on its own belly tile; in celebration, the copied emoji changes to a rocket. That synchronized body-and-tile change makes the emoji matching mechanic its superpower, rather than adding a loose accessory to an ordinary animal. Morphi offers the broadest future expression range, though each new copied emoji would need its own art state.

The two matching faces are the game objective made into a companion: in idle they hold hands, and after a win they jump into a high-five. A single view of the pair communicates both “emoji” and “match,” without relying on a species or narration. Their paired width is a cost in narrow UI slots, but that same shape would work well as a streak or level-completion banner.

## Production and recommendation

The artwork was made with the built-in image generation tool. Each celebration used its idle concept art as the identity reference; Flippo's idle pose and Bibi & Bobo's celebration received the listed targeted edits. Pillow cropped true-alpha artwork, fitted each subject within an 800 px box on a centered 1024 px canvas, and composed the comparison sheet with the same 340 px sprite canvas scale and gameplay crop used in round 1.

**Recommendation: Flippo.** It connects the exact question-mark card identity already on the icon to the actual apple emoji hidden inside the card. Its compact, high-contrast outline should survive repeated placement beside streaks and in the WinModal; the little reveal can grow with progress without redesigning the whole body.

**Against round 1's Miso:** Flippo gives up some of Miso's creature warmth, but its unmistakable card-and-emoji identity is worth that tradeoff for DoMemory's recurring mascot.
